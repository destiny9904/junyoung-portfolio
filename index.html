Case 01 Finda

The data everyone trusted was quietly wrong.

A regulator-approved dataset was the only legal source for deposit product terms. It didn't match what banks were actually offering, left without verification.

FINDA — Deposit/Savings Comparison Feature

Product Owner · Jun 2022 – Mar 2023
100+
Products verified
1
Data source, by law
Notice

Regulator data was the only source we were legally allowed to use.

Financial Services Commission testbed rules limited us to one government dataset.

→
Discover

Rates, terms, and minimums didn't match the banks' own listings.

Discrepancies showed up on the exact fields users would choose products by.

→
Verify at source

Checked every product directly against the institution.

100+ listings cross-checked with each bank; branch visits when online records weren't enough.

→
Ship & iterate

Found the bottleneck before shipping the fix everywhere.

Tracked the credit-management dashboard for where users dropped off, released the fix to a subset first, confirmed the metric moved, then rolled it out to everyone.

Still live

The comparison feature, largely unchanged since launch in 2022. Same verification logic, same product. Screens are in Korean; available on Finda app.

Scope

Every product on the platform listed: this is what 100+ verified listings look like to a user.

Output

Verified net return, side by side: the number a user can actually trust and act on.

Also translated investor-facing IR materials, cross-checking every figure, including exchange rates, against internal data before it shipped. Researched overseas SOHO-lending models for a B2B2C partnership with Woowa Brothers and Kakao Mobility, then analyzed platform-activity data against credit indicators to validate the approach.

Associate Product Manager (promoted from intern) · Jun 2020 – Aug 2021
5
Cold-outreach interviews
10,000
Subscribers, 4 months
Question

Which candidate country actually had a gap?

Compared Vietnam against other Southeast Asian markets on public data alone, with inconclusive results.

→
Go direct

Cold-messaged in-market professionals.

Reached Korean-company staff and local professionals already working in Vietnam.

→
Define the gap

Fast-growing industry, no localized content.

Vietnam's tech workforce was scaling with almost no in-language career content.

→
Ship the MVP

Launched a newsletter before building an app.

Validated demand cheaply, achieving 10K subscribers in 4 months, before committing engineering time.

→
Run it

Built onboarding, ran the team, hit the numbers.

Notion-based onboarding for new users; led 3 Vietnam-based growth interns + 2 Korean engineers on 2-week sprints, resolving bottlenecks through 1:1s and feeding them straight back into the quarterly plan.

Promoted from intern to Associate Product Manager at month 10. Subscriber and conversion data compiled into investor-facing materials during the company's Series B raise, and the launch was later featured in Hankyung Job&Joy (Jun 2021).

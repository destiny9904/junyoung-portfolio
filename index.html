Case 01 — Finda

The data everyone trusted was quietly wrong.

A regulator-approved dataset was the only legal source for deposit product terms. It didn't match what banks were actually offering — and nobody had checked.

Finda — Deposit Comparison Tool

Product Owner · Jun 2022 – Mar 2023
100+
Products verified
1
Data source, by law
Notice

Regulator data was the only source we were legally allowed to use.

Financial Services Commission testbed rules limited us to one government dataset.

→
Discover

Rates, terms, and minimums didn't match the banks' own listings.

Discrepancies showed up on the exact fields users would choose products by.

→
Verify at source

Checked every product against the institution directly.

100+ listings cross-checked with each bank; branch visits when online records weren't enough.

→
Ship & iterate

Released the verified product, then A/B-tested improvements.

Used an internal tool to control rollout across staging and production for credit-management features.

Also on this team: researched overseas SOHO-lending models for a B2B2C partnership with Woowa Brothers and Kakao Mobility, then analyzed platform-activity data against credit indicators to validate the approach.

Zero to one, in a market with almost no signal.

Which Southeast Asian market to enter wasn't obvious from any dashboard. The answer came from calling strangers, not reading reports.

Question

Which candidate country actually had a gap?

Compared Vietnam against other Southeast Asian markets on public data alone — inconclusive.

→
Go direct

Cold-messaged in-market professionals.

Reached Korean-company staff and local professionals already working in Vietnam.

→
Define the gap

Fast-growing industry, no localized content.

Vietnam's tech workforce was scaling with almost no career content in-language.

→
Ship the MVP

Launched a newsletter before building an app.

Validated demand cheaply — 10,000 subscribers in 4 months — before committing engineering time.

→
Run it

Built onboarding, ran the team, hit the numbers.

Notion-based onboarding for new users; led 3 Vietnam-based growth interns + 2 Korean engineers on 2-week sprints.

Promoted from intern to Associate Product Manager at 10 months. Subscriber and conversion data compiled into investor-facing materials during the company's Series B raise, and the launch was later featured in Hankyung Job&Joy (Jun 2021).
